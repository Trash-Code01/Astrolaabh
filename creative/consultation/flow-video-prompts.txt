01 | The visitor enters

Prompt:
One continuous over-the-shoulder shot in the Indian astrologer’s study shown in the first frame. The young man in the ivory linen shirt takes two measured steps across the sandstone threshold and slows at the teak desk. The camera follows just behind his shoulder at walking pace. Natural weight shifts, quiet expressions. Silent editorial film, no on-screen captions.

03 | The walk to the atelier
Start frame: 04-walk.jpg
Prompt:
A waist-height tracking shot of the same ivory-shirted visitor carrying the sealed olive envelope down the sandstone passage. He takes two natural steps toward the jeweller’s sunlit worktable. The camera tracks beside his hand. The envelope swings gently with his stride, with the flap, constellation print and gold compass seal facing camera. A leaf shadow moves across the paper as he crosses into sunlight. He slows and begins lifting the envelope toward the worktable. End while the envelope is still in his hand, before it touches the table. Preserve the seal shape and paper proportions. No dialogue, captions or music.
